# Arogya Shield Comprehensive Health Insurance Policy

**Policy Type:** Individual and Family Floater Health Insurance  
**Product Code:** ASH-2026-V1  
**Underwriter:** Arogya General Insurance Company Limited  

---

## 1. Plan Overview & Eligibility
* **Entry Age:** Adults: 18 years to 65 years. Dependent Children: 91 days to 25 years.
* **Renewability:** Lifelong renewability, subject to timely payment of premium.
* **Sum Insured Options:** Rs. 5,00,000 (Silver), Rs. 10,00,000 (Gold), and Rs. 25,00,000 (Platinum).
* **Policy Period:** 1 Year, 2 Years, or 3 Years (discount of 7.5% on 2-year and 12% on 3-year multi-year tenures).
* **Free Look Period:** 15 days from the date of receipt of the physical or digital policy document.

---

## 2. In-Patient Hospitalisation & Core Benefits
* **Room Rent & Boarding:**
  - *Silver Plan (Rs. 5L):* Capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day (Rs. 5,000/day). ICU capped at 2% per day (Rs. 10,000/day).
  - *Gold Plan (Rs. 10L) & Platinum Plan (Rs. 25L):* Single Private Air-Conditioned Room with **no proportionate deduction** or capping. No ICU sub-limits.
* **Pre-Hospitalisation Medical Expenses:** Covered up to **60 days** immediately prior to admission.
* **Post-Hospitalisation Medical Expenses:** Covered up to **90 days** immediately after discharge.
* **Day Care Procedures:** All 541+ day-care surgeries requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalisation due to technological advancement are covered up to Sum Insured.
* **Emergency Ambulance Cover:** Up to Rs. 3,000 per hospitalisation event.
* **AYUSH Treatment:** In-patient hospitalisation expenses incurred in recognized Government Ayurvedic, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathic institutions are covered up to 100% of the Sum Insured.

---

## 3. Waiting Periods
* **Initial Waiting Period:** **30 days** from policy inception date for any illness or hospitalization (accidental injuries are covered from Day 1).
* **Specified Named Diseases / Procedures:** **24 months** waiting period for hernia, cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hysterectomy, joint replacement (unless due to accident), and kidney stones.
  - *Cataract Sub-limit:* Rs. 40,000 per eye under Silver; Rs. 60,000 per eye under Gold and Platinum.
* **Pre-Existing Diseases (PED):** **36 months** continuous coverage waiting period before claims related to declared pre-existing diseases (including diabetes, hypertension, and asthma) are payable.
* **Maternity Benefit (Gold & Platinum Plans Only):** **24 months** waiting period. Capped at Rs. 50,000 for normal delivery and Rs. 75,000 for Caesarean section, up to 2 deliveries during the lifetime of the policy.

---

## 4. Cumulative Bonus (No-Claim Bonus)
* For every claim-free year, the Sum Insured increases by **10%**, up to a maximum accumulation of **50%** of the base Sum Insured.
* If a claim is made during any policy year, the accumulated cumulative bonus is reduced by 10% on renewal, but the base Sum Insured is never reduced.

---

## 5. Claims Process & Reimbursement
* **Network Hospitals (Cashless Settlement):**
  - Planned hospitalisation: Intimate insurer at least **48 hours** prior to admission.
  - Emergency hospitalisation: Intimate insurer within **24 hours** of admission.
  - Submit Health Card, government ID, and cashless pre-authorization form at the hospital TPA desk. Approvals processed within **2 hours** of final medical query resolution.
* **Non-Network Hospitals (Reimbursement Claim):**
  - Written notice of claim within **48 hours** of emergency admission or discharge.
  - Submit all original discharge summaries, itemized hospital bills, pharmacy receipts, and diagnostic reports within **15 days** of discharge.
  - Claim settlement or formal clarification within **30 days** of receiving complete documentation.

---

## 6. General Exclusions (What is NOT Covered)
The policy strictly excludes payment for:
1. **Cosmetic or Plastic Surgery:** Any surgery performed for aesthetic or beauty enhancement unless necessitated by an accidental burn or reconstructive cancer surgery.
2. **Adventure / Extreme Sports:** Any injury sustained while participating in hazardous or professional sports (including skydiving, mountaineering with ropes, motor racing, scuba diving, and paragliding).
3. **Substance Abuse & Self-Inflicted Injury:** Treatment arising from intentional self-injury, suicide attempts, or intoxication due to alcohol or non-prescribed drugs.
4. **Unproven / Experimental Treatments:** Treatments, therapies, or medications lacking recognized scientific verification by medical councils.
5. **Breach of Law:** Hospitalisation directly resulting from the insured person's participation in criminal or illegal activities.
6. **External Congenital Anomalies:** Inherited physical defects visible on the exterior body.

## 7. Premiums & Discounts
* **Sample Indicative Annual Premiums (Excluding 18% GST):**
  - Age 18–35 (Individual Rs. 5L): Rs. 6,800 / year.
  - Age 18–35 (Individual Rs. 10L): Rs. 9,400 / year.
  - Age 36–45 (Family Floater 2 Adults, Rs. 10L): Rs. 16,500 / year.
  - Age 46–55 (Family Floater 2 Adults + 1 Child, Rs. 10L): Rs. 24,200 / year.
* **Discounts:**
  - Family Floater Discount: 10% on adding 2 or more family members.
  - Multi-Year Policy Discount: 7.5% for 2-year policy; 12% for 3-year policy.
  - No Claim Discount: 5% renewal discount on premium if no claim was made in the preceding year.
* **Tax Benefit:** Premiums eligible for tax deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act up to Rs. 25,000 for self/family and an additional Rs. 50,000 for senior citizen parents.

---

## 8. Cancellation & Refund Terms
* **Free Look Period (Day 1 to 15):** 100% refund of premium, deducting stamp duty and medical check-up charges (if any).
* **Short-Period Refund Scale (After 15 days, provided no claim has been registered):**
  - Up to 1 month: 75% refund of annual premium.
  - Up to 3 months: 50% refund.
  - Up to 6 months: 25% refund.
  - Beyond 6 months: 0% refund.
